5. CARD ISSUANCE & FRAUD PREVENTION POLICY 
This policy outlines the issuance, usage, security, monitoring, interest charging rules, 
and dispute mechanisms governing Debit and Credit Cards issued by Secure Bank. 
 
5.1 Card Types Offered 
1. Debit Cards 
Linked directly to the customer's Savings or Current Account. 
Typical Numerical Limits 
• ATM Withdrawal Limit: ₹20,000 – ₹50,000 per day 
• POS/Online Purchase Limit: ₹50,000 – ₹2,00,000 per day 
• International FX Markup: 2% – 3.5% (if international usage enabled) 
• Annual Fee: ₹150 – ₹500 (variant -dependent) 
Features: 
• ATM, POS, UPI, NFC support 
• Zero-liability protection for fraudulent transactions reported within 24 hours 
 
2. Credit Cards (Gold / Platinum Variants) 
Credit facility offered with interest -based repayment. 
Typical Numerical Features 
• APR (Interest Rate): 30% – 42% per annum (2.5% – 3.5% per month) 
• Interest-Free Grace Period: 20–50 days 
• Late Payment Fee: ₹200 – ₹1,000 (tiered by bill amount) 
• Cash Advance Fee: 2.5% – 3% of withdrawn amount 
• FX Markup (International): 3% – 3.5% 
• Annual Fee: 
o Gold: ₹500 – ₹1,000 
o Platinum: ₹2,000 – ₹5,000 
• Minimum Due Payment: Usually 5% of outstanding balance 
Benefits: 
• Lounge access for Platinum 
• EMI conversion at 12% – 18% p.a. 
• Reward points (1 –5 points per ₹100 spent) 
 
5.2 Card Lifecycle Stages 
1. Application & Identity Verification 
• KYC + PAN mandatory 
• Credit Cards require: 
o Minimum Income Criteria: ₹20,000 – ₹40,000 per month 
o Minimum Credit Score: 700+ recommended 
 
2. Card Printing & Secure Dispatch 
• Printed at PCI -DSS compliant facility 
• Undelivered cards destroyed after 30–45 days 
 
3. PIN Generation 
• Mandatory OTP authentication 
• PIN reset validity checks every 6 months for security 
 
4. Transaction Monitoring (Real -Time) 
Fraud engine monitors: 
• High-value transactions: >₹25,000 
• Unusual spend spikes: 3+ transactions in 2 minutes 
• International attempts without prior enablement 
• Multiple incorrect PIN attempts (3 –5 times) → automatic temporary block 
 
5.3 Fraud Detection & Prevention Rules 
1. Geo-Location Mismatch Alerts 
Triggered if: 
• Transactions occur in 2 different cities/countries within 10–15 minutes 
2. Rapid Transaction Bursts 
• More than 5 attempts within 60 seconds 
• System triggers auto -block 
3. High-Value MCC Scrutiny 
Extra verification for transactions above: 
• Domestic: >₹50,000 
• International: >₹25,000 – ₹30,000 
4. Mandatory 2 -Factor Authentication (2FA) 
• OTP required for online transactions 
• International e -commerce uses 3 -D Secure or CVV -based approval 
5. Lost/Stolen Card Protocol 
• Blocking within 5 minutes of customer reporting 
• Replacement dispatched within 3–5 working days 
 
5.4 Chargeback & Dispute Resolution Policy 
1. Chargeback Request Window 
• Must be filed within 30 days of transaction 
• International card network timelines: 45–120 days depending on network 
2. Required Documents 
• Dispute form 
• Proof of non -participation 
• Transaction SMS / Statement excerpt 
3. Bank Responsibilities 
• Acknowledge within 24 hours 
• Provide provisional credit within 7–10 days, where applicable 
• Final resolution as per network SLA: 
o Visa/Mastercard: 45–90 days 
o Rupay: 30–60 days 
 
5.5 Record Retention 
• Card issuance, KYC, PIN logs, chargeback documents kept for minimum 10 
years 
• ATM CCTV footage retention: 90 days (industry standard) 
 
6. BRANCH OPERATIONS POLICY 
The Branch Operations Policy governs the daily functioning, safety standards, cash 
handling procedures, customer -facing operations, and backend administrative 
protocols across all Secure Bank branches. All branch personnel must strictly adhere 
to these guidelines to maintain operational integrity, ensure security, and comply with 
regulatory expectations. 
 
6.1 Teller Operations 
Tellers play a critical role in cash management and customer transactions. The 
following controls ensure secure and compliant handling of cash: 
1. Cash Holding Limits 
• Maximum cash that a teller may hold at any time: ₹2,00,000 . 
• Excess cash must be moved to the branch vault immediately through a 
documented cash transfer slip. 
2. Cash Replenishment 
• Cash received from the vault must be: 
o Verified by both teller and vault custodian 
o Logged in the Cash Replenishment Register 
o Reflected in the Core Banking System (CBS) 
• Replenishment must only be done during authorized time windows unless in 
emergencies approved by the Operations Manager. 
3. Dual Custody Protocol 
• Vault keys must always remain under dual custody : 
o Primary Key Holder (Operations Manager) 
o Secondary Key Holder (Designated Teller / Officer) 
• Keys must never be kept together or left unattended. 
• Access to the vault requires presence and signature of both custodians. 
 
6.2 Locker Operations 
Secure Bank provides safe deposit locker services under a regulated and 
audit-compliant framework. 
1. KYC Requirements 
• Locker allocation is permitted only after full KYC verification of the customer. 
• For joint locker holders, KYC must be completed for all applicants . 
• High-risk customers must undergo enhanced due diligence before allocation. 
2. Access Logging 
Every locker visit must be recorded in the Locker Access Register , capturing: 
• Date & time of access 
• Customer’s signature 
• Officer’s signature 
• Locker number 
• Any observations or irregular behavior 
Electronic logs (if available) must be reconciled weekly. 
3. Locker Break -Open Procedure 
A locker may be forced open under the following conditions: 
• Non-payment of rent despite due notices 
• Customer request (lost key) 
• Regulatory or court order 
Break-open must strictly follow RBI-mandated guidelines , including: 
• Presence of branch manager + authorized officer 
• Videography of the entire procedure 
• Detailed written inventory of locker contents 
• 7-day prior notice to customer (unless prohibited by law) 
 
6.3 End-of-Day (EOD) Procedures 
End-of-Day processes ensure accuracy, prevent financial discrepancies, and maintain 
audit readiness. 
1. Cash Tally 
• All cash drawers must be tallied with the CBS (Core Banking System) . 
• Variations (excess/short) must be: 
o Reported immediately 
o Recorded in the Cash Variation Register 
o Escalated to the Branch Manager 
2. Suspense Account Clearance 
• All suspense entries must be reviewed and cleared the same day . 
• Outstanding suspense items must be: 
o Documented with reason 
o Escalated if pending beyond 24 hours 
3. Audit Log Backup 
• System audit logs, transaction summaries, and CCTV retention logs must be: 
o Backed up to the secure central server 
o Verified by the Operations Manager 
o Stored in compliance with record -retention policy 
4. Physical Security Checks 
Before closing the branch: 
• All cash safes, strong room doors, and lockers must be secured. 
• Alarms and CCTV systems must be checked. 
• Visitor registers and manual logs must be closed and archived.
