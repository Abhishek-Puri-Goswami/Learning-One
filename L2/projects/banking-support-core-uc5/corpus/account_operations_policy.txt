3. ACCOUNT OPERATIONS POLICY 
The Account Operations Policy defines the standardized rules, financial parameters, 
and operational controls governing all deposit accounts at Secure Bank. These 
guidelines ensure transparent, compliant, and customer -centric handling of account 
lifecycle a ctivities across all branches. 
 
3.1 Permitted Account Types 
Secure Bank supports multiple categories of accounts tailored to diverse customer 
requirements. Each account type has specific eligibility criteria, minimum balance 
rules, interest calculations, and fee structures. 
 
1. Savings Account 
Designed for resident individuals seeking secure savings with moderate interest 
earnings. 
Key Features 
• Interest earned on daily closing balance; credited quarterly. 
• Typical interest range: 2.5% – 4% per annum (bank -declared, subject to change). 
• Minimum Average Balance (MAB): 
o Urban branches: ₹10,000 
o Semi-urban: ₹5,000 
o Rural: ₹2,000 
• MAB Penalties (illustrative): 
o Shortfall up to 25% → ₹100 fee 
o Shortfall 25 –50% → ₹150 fee 
o Above 50% → ₹200 fee 
• ATM withdrawal limit: ₹25,000 per day (may vary by card type). 
 
 
 
2. Current Account 
Intended for businesses requiring high -volume transactions. 
Key Features 
• No interest payable. 
• Higher transaction limits; NEFT/RTGS limits based on customer profile. 
• Minimum Monthly Average Balance: 
o Standard Current Account → ₹25,000 
o Premium Current Account → ₹50,000 
• Overdraft eligibility is case -to-case based on financials and collateral. 
 
3. Salary Account 
Zero-balance payroll account for salaried employees of empaneled organizations. 
Key Features 
• No minimum balance requirement. 
• Interest rate same as regular Savings Account. 
• Converts to a Savings Account after 90 days of salary inactivity. 
• Upon conversion, Savings Account MAB rules apply. 
 
4. Senior Citizen Account 
Special variant for customers aged 60 years and above. 
Key Features 
• Additional interest rate benefit: +0.50% on Savings & FD rates. 
• Cheque book issuance with reduced fees. 
• Higher ATM withdrawal limits (up to ₹40,000/day). 
• Lower MAB requirement: ₹5,000 (urban & semi -urban). 
 
5. NRI Account Types 
a. NRE (Non -Resident External) Account 
• Maintained in INR; fully repatriable. 
• Interest rate: 4% – 5.5%, tax -free in India. 
• Only foreign income deposits allowed. 
b. NRO (Non -Resident Ordinary) Account 
• For managing income earned in India. 
• Interest rate: 2.75% – 4% (subject to TDS). 
• Repatriation restricted to permitted limits. 
c. FCNR (Foreign Currency Non -Resident) Account 
• Fixed deposits maintained in foreign currency. 
• Tenure: 1 –5 years. 
• Interest rates vary by currency (USD, GBP, EUR, etc.). 
• Protection from currency fluctuation risk. 
 
3.2 General Rules Applicable to All Deposit Accounts 
1. Interest Calculation 
• Savings interest calculated on daily closing balance. 
• Credited quarterly or as per regulatory norms. 
• No interest on accounts classified as dormant. 
2. Minimum Balance Monitoring 
• Monitored monthly or quarterly based on account type. 
• Non-maintenance charges apply automatically. 
3. Dormant / Inactive Account Classification 
• Inactive after 12 months of no customer -initiated transactions. 
• Dormant after 24 months of inactivity. 
• Withdrawals from dormant accounts require enhanced verification. 
4. Transaction Limits 
• Cash deposit/withdrawal limits depend on branch jurisdiction, AML rules. 
• Electronic payments (NEFT/IMPS/RTGS) follow RBI/Regulator guidelines. 
5. Cheque Book Operations 
• Cheque books issued post -KYC validation. 
• Charges apply beyond free cheque leaves. 
6. Account Closure 
• Customer request + identity verification mandatory. 
• Dues, penalties, and charges adjusted before closure. 
 
3.3 Account Closure Policy 
Account closure must follow controlled, documented processes ensuring all customer 
liabilities and compliance checks are completed. 
1. Customer Request 
• A written and signed closure request is mandatory. 
• For joint accounts, all account holders must sign unless the mandate states 
otherwise. 
2. Pre-Closure Verification 
Before processing the closure, branch operations must confirm: 
• No pending charges or overdraft dues 
• No lien or freeze instructions 
• No ongoing investigation or suspicious transaction alert 
• No pending cheques or card settlements 
3. KYC Verification 
• If the last KYC update is older than 2 years , updated KYC is mandatory before 
closure (except where exempted by regulations). 
4. Settlement of Balance 
• Positive balances must be transferred to another account or provided via 
banker’s cheque/NEFT as requested. 
• For NRI customers, repatriation rules apply based on account type. 
5. Closure Confirmation 
• System must record closure reason. 
• Customer must receive: 
o Account closure acknowledgement 
o Final statement (if requested) 
 
3.4 Record Retention 
• Account opening forms, KYC documents, modification forms, and closure 
documents must be retained for 10 years , in line with regulatory requirements
