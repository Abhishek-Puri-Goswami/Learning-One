As part of Secure Bank’s ongoing commitment to excellence, transparency, and risk 
mitigation, this manual is periodically reviewed and updated to remain aligned with 
evolving Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines, national and international financial 
regulations, market practices, and internal audit findings. 
 
Objectives 
The primary objectives of the Secure Bank Policy & Operations Manual include, but are 
not limited to, the following: 
1. Regulatory Compliance 
• Ensure strict adherence to RBI regulations , Banking Regulation Act, KYC/AML 
guidelines, cybersecurity directives, consumer protection norms, and all 
applicable statutory requirements. 
• Provide employees with clear compliance steps to avoid legal violations, 
penalties, or reputational risks. 
2. Standardization of Internal Processes 
• Establish uniform procedures across all branches and business units for 
customer onboarding, account servicing, cash operations, lending, card 
issuance, digital banking, and grievance handling. 
• Reduce process deviations and ensure consistent customer experience 
regardless of geographic location. 
3. Strengthening Internal Controls 
• Define clear checks and balances for authorization, transaction verification, 
record-keeping, and audit readiness. 
• Minimize operational risks such as fraud, data breaches, financial 
misstatements, and process failures. 
• Promote a culture of accountability and controlled execution. 
4. Operational Efficiency 
• Streamline workflows to enhance service delivery speed, reduce turnaround 
times, and improve productivity. 
• Clearly define branch -level, department -level, and individual -level 
responsibilities to avoid duplication of work and confusion. 
5. Risk Management & Security 
• Incorporate proactive risk identification, mitigation, and monitoring 
mechanisms. 
• Ensure that physical, digital, financial, and informational assets are safeguarded 
from both internal and external threats. 
• Promote strong cybersecurity practices, customer data privacy, and secure 
transaction processing. 
6. Employee Guidance & Training 
• Provide employees —new and existing —with comprehensive procedural 
guidance to enhance skill levels and operational competency. 
• Support continuous learning through updated policy amendments, training 
schedules, and communication protocols. 
 
Scope 
The Secure Bank Policy & Operations Manual applies to the following entities, functions, 
and operational layers: 
1. Organizational Coverage 
• All Secure Bank branches , regional offices, head office departments, and 
corporate units. 
• Support divisions such as Operations, IT, HR, Risk, Compliance, Finance, 
Treasury, Customer Support, and Digital Banking. 
2. Personnel Coverage 
• All permanent and probationary employees of Secure Bank. 
• Contractual staff , outsourced personnel, business correspondents, and 
third-party service providers acting on behalf of the bank. 
• Temporary workers, interns, and consultants who handle bank data, systems, or 
customer interactions. 
3. Functional Coverage 
• Customer onboarding, KYC verification, and due diligence procedures. 
• Cash management, vault operations, teller activities, ATM operations, and cash 
reconciliation.

functions.

protocols.

4. Technology & Security Coverage

financial institutions. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
2.2 KYC Process Flow 
The following steps must be completed during onboarding: 
1. Customer Identity Verification (CIV) 
• Verification of identity documents using official databases / digital KYC methods. 
• Face-match verification (if applicable) through approved systems. 
2. Address Verification 
• Validation through documents such as Aadhaar, Passport, Driving License, or 
utility bills. 
• For non-residents, permitted foreign documents may be used. 
3. Screening Against Watchlists 
• Mandatory checks against: 
o Secure Bank internal risk lists 
o National watchlists 
o Global sanctions lists (UN, OFAC, etc.) 
• Any positive match must be escalated to the compliance officer. 
4. Customer Risk Categorization 
Customers are assigned a risk level based on profile, geography, occupation, 
transaction pattern, and screening results: 
• Low Risk – Salaried individuals, retirees 
• Medium Risk – Small business owners, self -employed 
• High Risk – Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs), high -value traders, foreign 
nationals in high -risk jurisdictions 
5. Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) 
Applied to high-risk customers , requiring: 
• Additional identity/address documents 
• Source of funds verification 
• Managerial approval before onboarding 
• Increased transaction monitoring 
 
2.3 Periodic KYC Requirements 
To ensure ongoing compliance: 
Risk Category KYC Update Frequency 
Low Risk Every 3 years 
Medium Risk Every 2 years 
High Risk Every 1 year or as required 
Additional periodic checks: 
• Re-screening against watchlists 
• Review of unusual transaction patterns 
• Verification of updated contact details
