4.1 Loan Categories 
Secure Bank offers multiple retail loan products. Each product includes specific 
interest bands, collateral rules, LTV limits, and fee structures (illustrative, generic 
values). 
 
a) Home Loan 
Typical Interest Rate Range: 8.25% – 10.75% p.a. (floating) 
Processing Fee: 0.25% – 0.50% of loan amount 
Maximum Tenure: Up to 30 years 
LTV (Loan -to-Value) Norms: 
• Property value < ₹30 lakh → up to 90% 
• ₹30–75 lakh → up to 80% 
• Above ₹75 lakh → up to 75% 
Key Conditions 
• Registered mortgage mandatory. 
• Property insurance coverage must be active. 
• EMI based on reducing balance method. 
 
b) Personal Loan 
Typical Interest Rate Range: 11.5% – 22% p.a. 
Processing Fee: 1% – 2.5% 
Tenure: Up to 5 years 
Risk Notes 
• Fully unsecured → requires strong credit score (700+). 
• CIBIL score below 650 may cause higher rate bands or reduced loan quantum. 
• FOIR limit generally ≤ 40% for personal loans. 
 
c) Auto Loan 
New Vehicle Loan Rate: 8.75% – 12.5% p.a. 
Used Vehicle Loan Rate: 11% – 16% p.a. 
Processing Fee: 0.5% – 1% 
LTV Limits 
• New four -wheeler: up to 85–90% of on-road price 
• Two-wheeler: up to 80–85% 
• Used vehicle: 60–70% of assessed value 
Key Conditions 
• Vehicle hypothecation until closure. 
• Mandatory valuation for pre -owned vehicles. 
 
d) Education Loan 
Interest Rate Range: 9% – 14% p.a. (linked to program/institution rating) 
Processing Fee: Nil to 1% (varies by scheme) 
Moratorium: Course period + 6–12 months 
Funding Coverage 
• Tuition fees, hostel fees, exam fees, books, equipment, travel (foreign). 
• Co-applicant(s) required for most students.

(No repetition; only numerical details added) 
a) Income Verification (Numerical Thresholds) 
• Minimum monthly net income (indicative): 
o Personal Loan: ₹20,000 – ₹30,000 
o Auto Loan: ₹18,000 – ₹25,000 
o Home Loan: ₹25,000+ (varies by location) 
b) Credit Score Requirements 
• Ideal score: 700+ 
• High-risk band: <650 → may require collateral/guarantor or enhanced rate. 
• Excessive credit enquiries (>4 –6 in last 6 months) trigger deeper scrutiny. 
c) FOIR Standards 
• Personal Loan: ≤ 40% 
• Auto Loan: ≤ 45% 
• Home Loan: ≤ 50% (flexible up to 55% if income is high and stable) 
d) Documentation SLA 
• KYC verification: within 24 –48 hours 
• FI report: within 3–5 working days

4.5 Loan Monitoring & Portfolio Management 
a) EMI Monitoring Thresholds 
• 1st EMI bounce → customer call + SMS 
• 2 consecutive bounces → account flagged as high-risk 
• 3 consecutive bounces → case forwarded to CMU 
b) SMA / NPA Classification Timeline 
• SMA-0: 1–30 days overdue 
• SMA-1: 31–60 days 
• SMA-2: 61–90 days 
• NPA: >90 days overdue 
c) Recovery Actions 
• Soft recovery: 0–60 days 
• Hard recovery/legal: >90 days
