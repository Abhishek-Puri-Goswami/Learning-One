8. CUSTOMER GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL POLICY 
Secure Bank is committed to providing transparent, fair, and timely resolution of all 
customer grievances. This policy ensures that every query, complaint, or dispute is 
handled efficiently through a structured escalation mechanism. All employees must 
follow this framework to maintain customer trust and meet regulatory expectations. 
 
8.1 Objective of the Policy 
• Ensure prompt and fair handling of customer complaints. 
• Provide multiple channels for customers to register grievances (branch, phone, 
email, website). 
• Comply with internal service standards and regulatory grievance redressal 
guidelines. 
• Monitor and analyze complaints to improve service quality. 
 
8.2 Resolution Timelines (Escalation Matrix) 
Complaints must be resolved within the specified Turn -Around-Time (TAT). If the 
customer is not satisfied or the complaint is not resolved within the stipulated time, it 
automatically escalates to the next level. 
Level 1: Branch Manager 
• TAT: 48 hours 
• Handles: 
o Basic service issues 
o Transaction -related queries 
o Branch staff behavior concerns 
Level 2: Regional Office 
• TAT: 5 working days 
• Handles: 
o Unresolved Level -1 complaints 
o Complaints involving inter -branch coordination 
o System-related or process -related delays 
Level 3: Nodal Officer 
• TAT: 30 days 
• Handles: 
o High-severity complaints 
o Regulatory escalations 
o Cases requiring specialized review 
• Final authority before escalation to: 
o Banking Ombudsman , where applicable 
 
8.3 Accepted Categories of Grievances 
The following types of customer complaints must be formally logged and resolved 
through the grievance management system: 
1. ATM-Related Failures 
• Cash not dispensed but debited 
• Partial dispensation 
• ATM downtime or technical errors 
2. Unauthorized Card Transactions 
• Card-not-present fraud 
• Skimming/Cloning attacks 
• Misuse of lost/stolen cards 
• Double charges or merchant errors 
3. Loan-Related Complaints 
• Delay in loan processing 
• Incorrect EMI calculations 
• Issues with disbursement 
• Miscommunication on interest rates or terms 
4. Staff Behaviour or Service Quality Issues 
• Rude or unprofessional staff behaviour 
• Delayed service response 
• Failure to follow standard operating procedures 
5. General Banking Issues 
• Cheque clearing delays 
• Account charges disputes 
• Mobile/Internet banking errors 
• Branch operational difficulties 
 
8.4 Customer Rights 
Secure Bank ensures that every customer is entitled to: 
1. Transparency 
• Clear communication about products, fees, and complaint status. 
• Access to the grievance redressal policy through the bank’s website and 
branches. 
2. Fair Treatment 
• No discrimination based on age, gender, religion, financial status, or literacy 
level. 
• Objective and unbiased investigation of every complaint. 
3. Timely Response 
• Acknowledgment of complaint within prescribed timelines. 
• Resolution within the defined TAT (as per escalation matrix). 
• Reasoned communication if additional time is required. 
 
8.5 Complaint Logging & Tracking 
• Complaints may be registered via: 
o Branch visit 
o Toll-Free Customer Care 
o Email: support@securebank.com 
o Online portal / Mobile banking 
• Each complaint receives a unique complaint reference number for tracking. 
• System reports must be reviewed weekly by Branch Managers and monthly by 
Regional Offices.
