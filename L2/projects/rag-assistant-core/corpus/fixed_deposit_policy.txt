11. FIXED DEPOSIT (FD) & TERM DEPOSIT POLICY 
The Fixed Deposit (FD) & Term Deposit Policy governs interest rate structures, tenure -
based returns, penalties, and premature withdrawal conditions for all deposit products 
at Secure Bank. These guidelines ensure consistency, transparency, and compliance 
across all branches . 
 
11.1 FD Product Overview 
Secure Bank offers multiple categories of Fixed/Term Deposits catering to resident 
individuals, senior citizens, and Non -Resident Indians (NRIs). Key attributes include: 
• Guaranteed returns for a defined tenure 
• Flexible tenure options 
• Higher interest rates for senior citizens (+0.50%) 
• Multi-currency options for FCNR deposits 
• Compliant with RBI interest rate and premature withdrawal norms

11.3 Premature Withdrawal Policy 
Premature withdrawal is permitted under certain conditions unless restricted by 
specific schemes. 
Rules: 
• No interest payable if withdrawn before 7 days . 
• Premature closure after minimum lock -in attracts a penalty of 0.5% – 1% on 
applicable interest rate. 
• Senior citizen bonus (+0.50%) is not applicable on prematurely closed deposits. 
• FCNR premature withdrawals follow RBI lock -in conditions (no interest if 
withdrawn before 1 year). 
 
11.4 Penalties & Violation Fees 
A. Premature Withdrawal Penalty 
• FD < 1 year: 0.50% penal adjustment 
• FD ≥ 1 year: 1.00% penal adjustment 
B. Minimum Balance Violation Fee (If Linked Savings Account Insufficient) 
(Taken from Savings MAB rules) Shortfall up to 25% → ₹100 
• 25%–50% shortfall → ₹150 
• Above 50% → ₹200 
C. Auto-Renewal Failure / Maturity Default 
• If customer’s instructions are missing, FD may auto -renew at prevailing rate . 
• Penalty of ₹200 for reversal of auto -renewal if done after 30 days. 
D. Missed Documentation / KYC Violations 
• FD cannot be renewed if KYC is outdated. 
• Delay beyond permissible period may lead to account restrictions . 
 
11.5 NRI-Specific FD Rules 
A. NRE Fixed Deposit 
• Interest: 4% – 5.5% p.a. 
• Fully repatriable 
• Tax-free interest in India ] 
B. NRO Fixed Deposit 
• Interest: 2.75% – 4% p.a. 
• Interest subject to TDS 
• Partially repatriable under RBI guidelines 
C. FCNR Deposit 
• Tenure: 1–5 years 
• Interest varies by currency (USD/GBP/EUR) 
• Currency fluctuation protection included 
 
11.6 Interest Calculation Rules 
• Calculated using daily/quarterly compounding as per scheme 
• Credited quarterly or at maturity 
• No interest for dormant or restricted accounts
